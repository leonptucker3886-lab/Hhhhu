## General Art Style Guidelines:
- **Art Style:** Professional cartoon illustration, Pixar/Disney inspired but with adult humor
- **Color Palette:** Warm oranges/yellows (campfire), deep greens (forest), muddy browns (earth), cool blues (lake)
- **Lighting:** Warm campfire glow, firefly sparkles, lake reflections
- **Details:** Include cookout elements (beer cans, grills, picnic tables, muddy ground)
- **Resolution:** High quality, suitable for game UI (256x256 or 512x512)
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background
- **Expression:** Each character should show their personality clearly in facial expression and body language
